GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Is audio to text really free?

Yes. This browser-based version is free, with no account or payment needed. The speech-recognition model downloads to your browser the first time you use it.

Are my recordings uploaded?

No. Files are decoded and transcribed on your device; this site does not upload your recording to a transcription server. The AI model itself is downloaded from Hugging Face on first use.

What formats and lengths are supported?

Common audio formats and browser-decodable MP4/WebM video are supported. The current version accepts files up to 75 MB and recordings up to 15 minutes. Browser codec support varies, particularly for video.

How accurate is the transcription?

Accuracy depends on recording quality, accents, background noise and the selected language. English mode is faster. Always review important transcripts, especially names and numbers. Some older devices may process slowly.
