Titleless

An experiment in making niche job search more useful.

Titleless.fyi is a project I'm building to explore what a better job-search experience could look like, starting with growth marketing.

The idea is to see whether better job sourcing, more thoughtful categorization, richer information, and useful search filters can make a relatively small, focused job board more valuable than one with thousands of listings.

It currently covers growth marketing roles across the US and Canada, including performance marketing, demand generation, lifecycle marketing, and marketing operations.

I'm continuing to experiment with job quality, search functionality, and how to make individual listings more useful to people evaluating their next role.
